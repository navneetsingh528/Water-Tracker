💧 Water Saving Tips

Small actions can save thousands of litres of water every year.

🚿 Shorter Showers

Reduce your shower by 5 minutes to save up to 30 litres daily.

🚰 Turn Off the Tap

Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth to save up to 12 litres.

🔧 Fix Leaks

A leaking tap can waste hundreds of litres every month.

🌧 Harvest Rainwater

Collect rainwater for gardening and cleaning purposes.

🧺 Full Load Washing

Run washing machines only with a full load to reduce water usage.

🌱 Water Plants Smartly

Water plants early in the morning or evening to reduce evaporation.

🌍 Did You Know?

Only about 2.5% of Earth's water is fresh water, and less than 1% is easily accessible for human use. Every drop matters!
